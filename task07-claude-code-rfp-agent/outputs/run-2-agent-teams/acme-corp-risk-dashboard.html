Top Risk Drivers

Five stacked legal BLOCKERS in the customer's first draft, plus one commercial BLOCKER, define the shape of this deal's risk:

  • §4.3 SLA + termination stacking — 99.99% uptime (above our 99.95% ceiling) combined with immediate termination and full refund on any miss of any duration. This compounding of an above-standard SLA target with a zero-tolerance termination trigger is the single most dangerous clause in the RFP — a transient blip could trigger total contract loss, not just a service credit.
  • §4.1 Uncapped liability & indemnification — voids our cyber insurance ceiling (24 months' fees) and extends to uninsurable regulatory-fine / reputational-damage exposure.
  • §4.2 Unannounced, vendor-paid audits up to 4x/year — open-ended operational cost and access exposure.
  • §4.4 Full IP assignment of all custom work product — fragments our reusable platform IP across every customer contract.
  • §4.5 Subprocessor veto at Acme's sole discretion — could unilaterally block operational changes.
  • §3.4 Unscoped MFN warranty — the only commercial-side BLOCKER; risks margin compression across our entire book, not just this deal.
